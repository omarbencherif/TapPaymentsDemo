🎯 SETUP & USE:

1. Update Credentials in src/config.js:

   export const TAP_CONFIG = {
     publicKey: 'pk_test_YOUR_KEY',     // ← Put your key here
     merchantId: 'YOUR_MERCHANT_ID',     // ← Put your ID here
     // ... rest of config
   };

2. Open: http://localhost:5173/

   ✅ SDK auto-initializes with your credentials
   ✅ Configuration section is collapsible (hidden by default)
   ✅ Click the "🔑 Tap SDK Configuration" header to expand if needed

3. Test CIT Payment (Step 1):
   - Card: 4111 1111 1111 1111
   - CVV: 123
   - Expiry: 12/26
   - Name: Test User
   - Check "Save card for later"
   - Click "Pay & Save Card"
   - ✅ Card tokenized & saved!

4. See Saved Cards (Step 2):
   - Your card appears as a visual credit card
   - Shows brand, last 4 digits, expiry

5. Test MIT Payment (Step 3):
   - Select saved card from dropdown
   - Enter amount
   - Click "Pay with Saved Card"
   - ✅ Instant payment (no 3DS)!
